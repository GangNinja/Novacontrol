## Findings and limitations

- **Route load:** all 25 routes were reachable after the readiness-wait repair. The two workbench pages retain a loading placeholder in one panel while their controls render; the matrix records the route as loaded from the rendered `h1` and records the placeholder separately rather than calling the route dead.
- **Disabled pagination:** `Previous` is correctly disabled on the first Market page; the targeted after-`Next` check is recorded under Extra checks.
- **Refresh controls:** the six Refresh buttons (Trading Watch, Portfolios short/long, Transactions, Ledger, Health) were re-probed with a 12-second network window. Each issued its authoritative request and received a response; the current rows are PASS with the observed requests and responses attached. See `.ui_audit/refresh_probe.json`.
- **Data-dependent forms:** Paper `Submit from proposal` opens and validates, but its submit action remains disabled because the isolated fixtures contain no approved proposal. That correct safe state is a PASS for the form, not an order submission.
- **Filter loading:** Market's Industry select was sampled before its data-derived options arrived during the generic field sweep; the closure retest observed 23 options, selected `Automobile and Auto Components`, and Reset restored all. OrderBlock's sector select exposed 19 options in its retest. No unresolved single-option control is counted.
- **Secrets:** the API-key dialog was opened and closed without a value; Settings used a synthetic session token and cleared it. No backend credential was read, printed, or stored.
- **Repeated controls:** repeated row buttons and row-selectors are grouped with a count; one representative was exercised. The result for that component is not duplicated 126 times.
- **Forms not submitted:** strategy, backtest, shadow-session, forecast, portfolio-book, API-key, and lifecycle/destructive confirmations were opened and field-validated but not submitted. The write-flow section lists only the mutations actually executed against the isolated copy.
- **Watchlists:** the current page exposes create, rename, select, add-instrument and remove-membership. Rename was exercised through the real modal and is recorded from `closure_ui.json`; destructive list deletion is not exposed and is not claimed.
- **Derivatives:** official NSE futures and index-option snapshots are available from the packaged bhavcopy; provider-reported option IV is unavailable, while model-derived IV is labelled as such. Equity-scanner mapping is reported separately from the futures snapshot; no synthetic contract master was created. The 4,535-instrument scan belongs to the isolated audit database, not the 126-bar live API fixture.
